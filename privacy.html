RoboCup Logs · Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy describes the RoboCup Logs uploader operated by the repository maintainer, parsa600.

Information processed and purpose

The uploader processes selected RoboCup match logs, simulation recordings, reports and archive metadata to store and share simulation results. Google Drive file and folder identifiers, names, sizes and timestamps may be processed to locate the destination, upload archives, obtain links and apply retention settings.

Google authorization

rclone uses OAuth access and refresh tokens to act on the authorized Google Drive account. The permission screen shows the requested access. If full Drive access is granted, that permission technically permits access beyond the destination folder; the workflow is intended to operate on its configured log destination. Do not authorize access unless you trust the workflow and runner.

Credentials are handled by the operator's rclone configuration or workflow secret configuration. They must not be committed to this public repository. This documentation website does not receive or store them.

Storage, recipients and sharing

Archives are stored in the selected Google Drive account. Temporary logs and archives are also processed on the runner; workflow output, reports and link artifacts may be stored by GitHub. Access follows the settings of those services and the operator's repository and runner.

The upload workflow can create an anyone-with-the-link download link. When enabled, anyone who obtains that link may download the archive. Such links may appear in workflow summaries or GitHub artifacts. Files are not automatically published on this documentation website.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on workflow settings. The current uploader defaults to cleanup of matching log archives older than 30 days; the operator can change or disable that setting. Google Drive trash, GitHub artifact retention and runner diagnostics have separate retention behavior. Deleting a Drive file does not automatically delete other copies or GitHub artifacts.

Your controls

You may revoke the app's access through your Google Account's third-party connections settings, remove sharing permissions, delete uploaded files in Drive, and ask the operator to remove runner copies and GitHub artifacts. Revoking authorization prevents future authorized access but does not itself delete existing files or disable existing share links.

Use restrictions

Google user data is used for the file-transfer, sharing and retention functions described here, not for advertising, sale, or training AI models. RoboCup Logs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website and contact

This static website uses no application analytics, advertising or sign-in scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process connection information under GitHub's own privacy policy. For questions, contact the maintainer through the support link below without posting confidential data. Policy changes will be published here with an updated effective date.
